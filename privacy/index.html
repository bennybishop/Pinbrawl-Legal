Privacy Policy

Pinbrawl for Android / Google Play

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Current scope: This policy describes Pinbrawl's current offline Android test build. It stores progress on your device and does not require a player account. TinySauce analytics and associated third-party measurement are disabled in that build. A future build that enables additional processing will need updated disclosures before that processing begins.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Benjamin Dehant, developer of Pinbrawl, is the controller responsible for personal information handled directly by the developer, including support and privacy requests.

Contact: benjamin.dehant@gmail.com.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers Pinbrawl for Android and these legal pages. Publication and testing are managed through the Voodoo Publishing Platform. Use of that platform does not by itself mean that gameplay data is transmitted from the current offline build.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its pages contain no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, embedded third-party content, or forms. GitHub nevertheless processes the network information necessary to serve the website, as described below.

3. Information processed

Game information stored on your device

Pinbrawl stores progress, completed tutorials, battle checkpoints, upgrades, hero and ball collection levels, in-game currency balances, and settings such as sound and reduced motion in the app's local storage. A local recovery copy may also be kept. The current build does not upload this save data to a developer-operated server.

You do not need to provide your name, email address, or a social-media login to play. The current build has no player-account creation or Facebook Login feature. It does not use contacts, photos, messages, microphone recordings, or precise GPS location for gameplay.

Support and privacy correspondence

If you email the developer, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments or technical details you choose to provide. This information is used to answer you and handle the request. Please provide only relevant information and avoid passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Distribution and website access

Google processes information when you use Google Play to obtain or update the app, under its own privacy policy and your Google settings. Distribution through Google Play does not mean that the developer receives your Google account details.

When you visit these pages, GitHub receives information needed for hosting and security, such as your IP address and request information. Email correspondence is also handled by the email providers involved in delivery.

4. Purposes of processing

  • Keep your progress, collection, and preferences available on your device and recover a local save where possible.
  • Respond to support, deletion, and other privacy requests you initiate.
  • Protect correspondence and this website from misuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

The current offline test build does not send gameplay analytics or advertising-attribution events to TinySauce, GameAnalytics, or Meta. The developer does not sell personal information.

5. Legal bases and privacy choices

Where the GDPR applies, the developer relies on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) to respond to support correspondence and maintain appropriate records of requests, balanced against your rights. Handling a statutory privacy request and maintaining records required by law rely on Article 6(1)(c). Information necessary to provide a service you specifically request may be processed under Article 6(1)(b), where applicable.

Local progress is used only to provide gameplay and save functionality in the current build. You can remove local information using Android's app-storage controls. No optional analytics consent is needed for the disabled services in the current offline test build.

If a later build introduces optional analytics, attribution, or advertising, it must explain the specific information, purposes, providers, legal bases, and applicable consent choices before enabling that processing. Where processing depends on consent, you may withdraw it as easily as giving it; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

6. Third-party services and current status

  • Voodoo / TinySauce: Voodoo's publishing platform is used for publication and testing. TinySauce is integrated into the development project, but is disabled in the current offline test APK. No active in-app analytics or advertising processing by TinySauce is claimed here. Voodoo Privacy Policy.
  • GameAnalytics: bundled with TinySauce in the development project; gameplay-event reporting is disabled in the current offline test APK. GameAnalytics Privacy Notice.
  • Meta App Events: not enabled in the current offline test APK. There is no Facebook Login in that build. These pages do not claim that Meta receives player information from it. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play: Android distribution and updates, when you obtain the app through Google Play. Google handles its platform information under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting and delivery of these legal pages, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The developer's contact address uses Gmail; Google processes email delivery and storage under the applicable Google terms and privacy notice. Providers' roles as processors or independent controllers depend on the relevant service. No unspecified future SDK collection is authorised by this policy.

7. International transfers

Providers involved in email delivery, website hosting, and Google Play may process information outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Their notices explain the applicable locations and safeguards. Where the developer arranges a restricted transfer, an appropriate legal mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses, is required. You may contact the developer for information about safeguards relevant to your request.

Local game saves in the current offline build are not transferred by the developer. Android or device-backup services, if enabled by you, are governed by the relevant platform settings and policies.

8. Retention and deletion

Local game information remains until it is overwritten, reset, or removed through Android's app-storage controls or uninstallation, subject to device-backup behaviour. The developer cannot remotely erase a save stored only on your device.

Support correspondence is kept while needed to resolve your request and any necessary follow-up. Relevant records may be retained longer where a specific legal obligation or the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims requires it. Unnecessary correspondence should be deleted; no fixed retention period for a future analytics service is asserted here.

Google Play and GitHub retain information they control according to their own notices. See the User Data Deletion page for the request process, its scope, and any applicable retention exceptions.

9. Children's privacy

The current build does not ask players for their age or identity and does not enable the optional measurement services described above. If you are a parent or guardian and believe personal information about a child has been sent to the developer, please contact the developer to investigate and, where appropriate, delete it. Do not send unnecessary identifying information about a child.

The intended audience and any age-related restrictions must be confirmed for the applicable Google Play release. This policy does not assert a minimum age or a child-directed status that has not been verified.

10. Your privacy rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions in applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, or portability of your personal information. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent where consent is the basis. You may also complain to the supervisory authority in your country of residence, workplace, or the place of the alleged infringement.

To make a request, email benjamin.dehant@gmail.com. Only proportionate information will be requested to verify your request and locate any relevant records. The developer cannot identify a local-only save from your email address.

For GDPR requests, the developer will respond without undue delay and normally within one month of receipt. If a lawful extension of up to two additional months is necessary because of the complexity or number of requests, you will be informed within the first month, with reasons. If a request cannot be fulfilled, the response will explain why and the available complaint rights.

11. Security

The developer uses reasonable measures appropriate to the information handled, including limiting information requested for support and keeping game saves within the app's local storage. These pages are served over HTTPS. Local recovery files help protect progress against corruption; they are not a claim of encryption. No system provides absolute security.

12. Changes and release-specific confirmation

This policy will be updated when the game's actual processing changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. Significant changes will be communicated through appropriate in-app or distribution notices when required.

Release details requiring confirmation: the final Google Play build's active SDKs and network behaviour; any advertising or attribution; exact data categories, recipients, retention, transfer safeguards, and consent controls; intended audience and age settings; and any account, online multiplayer, or cloud-save feature. Until verified, these must not be treated as active features or confirmed data collection. The present policy describes the offline test build, not an unverified future release.
