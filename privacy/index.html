Privacy Policy

Pinbrawl for Android / Google Play

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Builds covered: Pinbrawl is planned to use Voodoo's TinySauce SDK in its Android publishing and test builds. This policy explains the local game information and the SDK processing described below. Earlier offline test APKs omit or disable TinySauce; SDK processing does not take place in those APKs. Availability of a particular service depends on the build, its actual configuration, applicable law, and your privacy choices.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Benjamin Dehant, developer of Pinbrawl, is the controller responsible for personal information handled directly by the developer, including support and privacy requests.

Contact: benjamin.dehant@gmail.com.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers Pinbrawl for Android and these legal pages. Publication and testing are managed through the Voodoo Publishing Platform. SDK-enabled versions use TinySauce for publishing analytics, gameplay measurement, and consent management. These functions are distinct from any optional advertising or attribution service, which is described only where enabled.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its pages contain no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, embedded third-party content, or forms. GitHub nevertheless processes the network information necessary to serve the website, as described below.

3. Information processed

Game information stored on your device

Pinbrawl stores progress, completed tutorials, battle checkpoints, upgrades, hero and ball collection levels, in-game currency balances, and settings such as sound and reduced motion in the app's local storage. A local recovery copy may also be kept. Local save files are not uploaded as a cloud-save feature by the current game integration. In SDK-enabled versions, selected gameplay events may nevertheless be transmitted for analytics; a local save and an analytics event are different records.

You do not need to provide your name, email address, or a social-media login to play. The current build has no player-account creation or Facebook Login feature. It does not use contacts, photos, messages, microphone recordings, or precise GPS location for gameplay.

TinySauce and gameplay measurement in SDK-enabled builds

When TinySauce and its analytics providers are enabled, the integration can process the following categories for the functions actually configured:

  • Technical information: app and SDK versions, Android version, device manufacturer and model, language or locale, screen resolution, platform, and connection type.
  • Installation and session information: pseudonymous app-instance or installation identifiers, session identifiers, event times, session counts and duration, and first-launch information.
  • Gameplay events: game or level starts and finishes, level or match progression, scores, and win/loss outcomes. These are the progression events wired into the integration; other custom events are not assumed to be collected merely because an SDK supports them.
  • Network information: IP address received when an enabled service is contacted and, where used by that service, an approximate country or region inferred from network information. This is not access to precise GPS location.
  • Privacy choices: analytics and advertising/attribution consent decisions and related preference records.

If advertising measurement or attribution is enabled and permitted by your choices and device settings, the relevant service may additionally process an Android advertising identifier, install referrer or campaign information, and configured install or app-event signals to measure where players came from. This does not mean that Pinbrawl displays advertisements. Ad serving, purchases, social login, and cloud saves are not implied by installing TinySauce.

The developer and enabled publishing or analytics partners may receive event records or reports to understand sessions, progression, and outcomes. These pseudonymous records can still be personal data; they are not described as anonymous solely because they do not contain your name.

Support and privacy correspondence

If you email the developer, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments or technical details you choose to provide. This information is used to answer you and handle the request. Please provide only relevant information and avoid passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Distribution and website access

Google processes information when you use Google Play to obtain or update the app, under its own privacy policy and your Google settings. Distribution through Google Play does not mean that the developer receives your Google account details.

When you visit these pages, GitHub receives information needed for hosting and security, such as your IP address and request information. Email correspondence is also handled by the email providers involved in delivery.

4. Purposes of processing

  • Keep your progress, collection, and preferences available on your device and recover a local save where possible.
  • In SDK-enabled builds, measure sessions, progression, scores, and game outcomes to assess tests and improve balancing, onboarding, and performance.
  • Record and respect privacy choices. If separately enabled, measure installs and campaign attribution under the relevant choices.
  • Respond to support, deletion, and other privacy requests you initiate.
  • Protect correspondence and this website from misuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

SDK processing applies only to the services actually enabled in your version. Earlier offline test builds do not send these SDK events. The developer does not sell personal information.

5. Legal bases and privacy choices

Where the GDPR applies, the developer relies on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) to respond to support correspondence and maintain appropriate records of requests, balanced against your rights. Handling a statutory privacy request and maintaining records required by law rely on Article 6(1)(c). Information necessary to provide a service you specifically request may be processed under Article 6(1)(b), where applicable.

Local progress is used to provide gameplay and save functionality. You can remove it using Android's app-storage controls. Optional gameplay analytics in the planned TinySauce integration rely on consent under Article 6(1)(a), with the app's analytics-event forwarding gated on the analytics consent decision. Where consent is required for advertising identifiers, attribution, or Meta measurement, it must be obtained before that processing starts. Declining optional measurement does not require you to provide a name or create an account.

SDK-enabled builds must provide privacy or consent controls so you can review and change your choices. You can also contact the developer for help or to exercise your rights. Where processing depends on consent, you may withdraw it as easily as giving it; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. Resetting an Android advertising identifier is not the same as deleting earlier service records. No blanket legitimate-interest basis is asserted here for every SDK function.

6. Third-party services and build-dependent processing

  • Voodoo / TinySauce: planned for SDK-enabled publishing and test builds, for in-app analytics, publishing measurement, and privacy-choice management. Voodoo receives information processed by its enabled services; its role and purposes are explained in the Voodoo Privacy Policy.
  • GameAnalytics: the gameplay-analytics provider bundled with TinySauce, used when enabled and after the required analytics consent. The integration forwards progression starts, completions/failures, and scores. See the GameAnalytics data and privacy information. The developer is the contact for requests concerning Pinbrawl records.
  • Meta App Events: only if Meta measurement is actually enabled in the released build. Its purpose is app-event and advertising-campaign measurement, using the configured signals and permitted identifiers. There is no Facebook Login in the current game integration; App Events does not itself require one. Meta's activation and event settings still require confirmation. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Attribution: the TinySauce project includes Adjust configuration. Adjust is a potential recipient only if its attribution service is enabled for the build, for install and campaign measurement. Its activation and exact fields must be confirmed before collection. Adjust Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play: Android distribution and updates, when you obtain the app through Google Play. Google handles its platform information under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting and delivery of these legal pages, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The developer's contact address uses Gmail; Google processes email delivery and storage under the applicable Google terms and privacy notice. Providers' roles as processors or independent controllers depend on the relevant service. A provider being listed does not mean every optional feature it offers is enabled. The final provider configuration must match these disclosures and the in-app partner list.

7. International transfers

Enabled publishing, analytics, and attribution providers, as well as providers involved in email delivery, website hosting, and Google Play, may process information outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Their notices explain the applicable locations and safeguards. Where the developer arranges a restricted transfer, an appropriate legal mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses, is required. You may contact the developer for information about safeguards relevant to your request.

Local game saves in the current offline build are not transferred by the developer. Android or device-backup services, if enabled by you, are governed by the relevant platform settings and policies.

8. Retention and deletion

Local game information remains until it is overwritten, reset, or removed through Android's app-storage controls or uninstallation, subject to device-backup behaviour. The developer cannot remotely erase a save stored only on your device.

Support correspondence is kept while needed to resolve your request and any necessary follow-up. Relevant records may be retained longer where a specific legal obligation or the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims requires it. Unnecessary correspondence should be deleted.

SDK event and identifier records are retained only for the measurement purposes described above and any applicable legal requirements, according to the enabled service's settings and terms. Exact Pinbrawl account-level retention settings have not yet been confirmed; no unsupported fixed period is promised. You can request the applicable retention information and deletion through the developer. Genuinely anonymised statistics may remain after identifiable records are removed.

Google Play and GitHub retain information they control according to their own notices. See the User Data Deletion page for the request process, its scope, and any applicable retention exceptions.

9. Children's privacy

The current game integration does not ask players for their age or identity. The audience and age-appropriate SDK settings for the publishing build remain to be confirmed before optional measurement is enabled. Voodoo's current notice sets an age condition for its apps and games; the applicable publishing terms and Google Play audience settings must be checked rather than assuming that all ages are eligible. If you are a parent or guardian and believe personal information about a child has been sent to the developer, please contact the developer to investigate and, where appropriate, delete it. Do not send unnecessary identifying information about a child.

The intended audience and any age-related restrictions must be confirmed for the applicable Google Play release. This policy does not assert a minimum age or a child-directed status that has not been verified.

10. Your privacy rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions in applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, or portability of your personal information. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent where consent is the basis. You may also complain to the supervisory authority in your country of residence, workplace, or the place of the alleged infringement.

To make a request, email benjamin.dehant@gmail.com. Only proportionate information will be requested to verify your request and locate any relevant records. SDK information may be associated with a pseudonymous installation or device identifier, so an email address alone may not identify it. The developer will explain any limited extra details needed. The developer cannot identify a local-only save from your email address.

For GDPR requests, the developer will respond without undue delay and normally within one month of receipt. If a lawful extension of up to two additional months is necessary because of the complexity or number of requests, you will be informed within the first month, with reasons. If a request cannot be fulfilled, the response will explain why and the available complaint rights.

11. Security

The developer uses reasonable measures appropriate to the information handled, including limiting information requested for support and keeping game saves within the app's local storage. Enabled SDK network handling and consent controls must be checked in the publishing build. These pages are served over HTTPS. Local recovery files help protect progress against corruption; they are not a claim of encryption. No system provides absolute security.

12. Changes and release-specific confirmation

This policy will be updated when the game's actual processing changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. Significant changes will be communicated through appropriate in-app or distribution notices when required.

Release details requiring confirmation: the final Google Play build's active SDKs and network behaviour; any advertising or attribution; exact data categories, recipients, retention, transfer safeguards, and consent controls; intended audience and age settings; and any account, online multiplayer, or cloud-save feature. Until verified, these must not be treated as active features or confirmed data collection. The TinySauce-enabled processing described above is planned; this policy does not certify that a future build has passed those checks. Earlier offline test APKs remain covered by the narrower, local-only behaviour described here.
