User Data Deletion

Instructions for Pinbrawl

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Pinbrawl does not currently require a player account. In TinySauce-enabled publishing or test builds, analytics and any separately enabled measurement services may hold information under pseudonymous installation or device identifiers. Earlier offline test APKs omit or disable those services. You can request deletion of personal information held for the version you use, including SDK records where applicable and correspondence sent to the developer.

Request deletion

You can request deletion of personal data associated with Pinbrawl by emailing the developer:

Email a deletion request

If the button does not open your email app, write to benjamin.dehant@gmail.com with the subject Pinbrawl - Data Deletion Request.

What to include

Start with the game name and the email address or correspondence reference relevant to your request. The following technical details are optional and should be provided only if relevant or requested to locate a record:

  • the game name: Pinbrawl;
  • your country or region, only if relevant to the request;
  • your device model and Android version;
  • the approximate dates when you installed and last played the game;
  • a reference to any previous support message and any in-app support or consent identifier, if available and relevant; and
  • whether you want deletion of all associated data or only a specific category.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification, precise location, or other sensitive information. Do not send your complete Android advertising identifier unless the developer specifically asks for it through an appropriate follow-up process.

What happens next?

  1. The developer will acknowledge your request and may ask for limited additional information if the records cannot be located.
  2. The request will be verified to a reasonable extent so another person cannot delete data that belongs to you.
  3. Personal information controlled by the developer and covered by your request, such as identifiable support correspondence and relevant attachments, will be deleted where required. If a specific record must be retained to meet a legal obligation or establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim, the developer will explain the reason and applicable period or retention criteria. Truly anonymous information is not personal data.
  4. If your particular build uses a provider that holds relevant information, the developer will explain the available deletion route and coordinate with that provider where responsible for doing so. For TinySauce-enabled builds, this may involve Voodoo and GameAnalytics, and Meta or an attribution provider only if enabled. Earlier offline APKs do not create these SDK records. The developer will explain which recipients are relevant to your request.
  5. For GDPR requests, you will receive a response without undue delay and normally within one month of receipt. If complexity or the number of requests justifies an extension of up to two further months, the developer will explain this within the first month. If a request is refused, the response will explain the reasons and complaint rights.

No Pinbrawl account is currently created, so there is no developer-held account to close. The developer cannot remotely delete a local-only game save. If a record cannot be reliably located, the developer will explain what can be done without asking for unnecessary identification.

Delete local game data from Android

You can separately remove progress and settings stored on your device:

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Open Apps and select Pinbrawl.
  3. Open Storage or Storage & cache.
  4. Choose Clear storage or Clear data.

Uninstalling the game also removes ordinary local app data, subject to Android or cloud-backup settings. Clearing or uninstalling the app does not by itself delete information already processed by an enabled third-party SDK; use the email process above for that request.

Privacy choices and SDK records

In SDK-enabled builds, use the available privacy or consent controls to change optional analytics and advertising/attribution choices. Withdrawal stops future processing that relies on that consent; request deletion separately for earlier identifiable records. Clearing app storage, uninstalling, or resetting an advertising identifier does not automatically erase server records. If you no longer have the app, you can still use the email request route.

Platform data and Meta requests

This page is also the contact route for a request concerning Pinbrawl and Meta App Events, if you believe a particular version processed your information. Include the app version if known. Meta App Events is relevant only in a build that actually enables it; Facebook Login is not part of the current game integration. This site provides deletion instructions and is not an automated Meta deletion callback.

Clearing Pinbrawl's data does not delete your Google account, Google Play history, GitHub hosting logs, or other information independently controlled by a platform. The developer can explain relevant routes, but cannot promise deletion of records outside the developer's control. See the provider links in the Privacy Policy.
